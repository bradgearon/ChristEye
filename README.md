## The vision

**ChristEye** is a cross-disciplinary research framework for understanding how living and physical systems are structured, communicate, develop, remember, and adapt. It combines systems engineering, neuroscience, cellular biology, molecular signaling, and experimental modeling with a restoration-centered philosophy: **understand how a system works, then explore how to preserve or improve its function.**

The **Living Periodic Table** is a functional map of proposed relationships across domains—not a replacement for the chemical periodic table or a claim that all entries are scientifically equivalent.

## The ChristEye framework

| Principle | What it represents |
|:--|:--|
| **Soul** | The organized whole: identity, unity, and continuity |
| **Marrow** | Inherited structure, genetics, and developmental potential |
| **Lotus** | Coordination, signaling interpretation, and adaptive response |
| **Winds** | Information flow: chemical, electrical, and other measurable signals |
| **Roots** | Persistent history, connections, and learned state |
| **Heart** | Needs, homeostasis, priorities, and protection |

**Understanding** is the useful response a system learns to produce; **Fruit** is the observable result. *Milk* and *Honey* name proposed complementary families of metabolic, ionic, recognition, and stabilization processes—not literal feeding instructions or established standalone mechanisms.

## What we're exploring

- **Living memory & biological computing** — Neuronal engrams, astrocytes, ARC-associated RNA transport, IGFBP2, synaptic plasticity, and biohybrid interfaces.
- **Knowledge Genesis, Join & Transfer** — Whether developing neural networks can acquire, share, and independently retain specific learned responses; including blood-derived neural-cell research concepts and molecular-state reconstruction.
- **Sylph: restorative biological systems** — Noninfectious viral-binding and adaptive-control models, plus exploratory cancer-surveillance concepts.
- **Physics & adaptive materials** — Testable systems models, the *CW-09 Rooted Horizon* analogue-horizon simulation, and speculative material-process research.
- **Restoration-oriented engineering** — Turning cross-domain analogies into measurable variables, falsifiable predictions, controls, and reproducible experiments.
